🥤 Smoothie Prep Tips
1
Pull the cup from the freezer the moment you wake up
Everything frozen together = one solid puck on the blade. Set it on the counter first thing and let it sit while you shower and get ready — 5–10 min is all it needs.
2
Don't fill the cup past halfway when prepping it the night before
You're adding milk in the morning on top of frozen ingredients. If the cup is already full of frozen stuff, milk has nowhere to go and it overflows when blending.
3
Add milk slowly — stop before the MAX line
Pour milk to just below the MAX fill line. Frozen ingredients + liquid expand into a vortex — going over the line is what causes the overflow.
4
Swap whole almonds for almond butter only
Whole almonds bounce off the blade and never fully break down. Almond butter blends smoothly and gives you the same nutrition — add a spoonful to the cup when prepping.
5
Blend 45 seconds straight
One continuous run beats pulsing every time. Let it go the full 45 sec without stopping.

The 2-minute task start rule

When you sit down and feel paralyzed: write down the single next physical action. Not "work on the model" — but "open the Excel file and type the revenue assumption in cell B4." Then do only that. Two minutes. You are not committing to finishing. You are committing to one tiny, non-threatening action. Once started, initiation anxiety drops ~80%.
